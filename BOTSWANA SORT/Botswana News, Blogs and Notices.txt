#### [NEWS] Item 1: *Parliament Discloses P4 Billion Alcohol Levy Collections and Funding for Serowe In-Patient Rehab Center*

* **Type:** News Article
* **Date:** April 9, 2025
* **Source:** *DailyNews Botswana (BOPA)*
* **Topic:** Addictive Substances & Healthcare Infrastructure
* **Full Context & Details:**
In a parliamentary session addressing substance use policy, the Assistant Minister of Health, Mr. Lawrence Ookeditse, disclosed that the Botswana Unified Revenue Services (BURS) collected P4,006,029,956.16 through the alcohol levy. The Assistant Minister clarified that the Ministry of Health receives 10 percent of these funds for direct anti-alcohol interventions. Between 2018 and 2023, the ministry received P214.8 million from the levy, expending P166.2 million on health programs. The report highlighted that the number of citizens receiving treatment through alcohol levy interventions rose from 668 in 2017 to a cumulative 3,006. Minister Ookeditse emphasized that substance use disorder remains a chronic relapsing brain disease requiring continuous family and legislative support. To expand clinical capacity, the ministry confirmed construction of an in-patient rehabilitation center at the old Sekgoma Memorial Hospital Institute of Health Sciences in Serowe. The 50-bed facility—featuring 30 beds for adults (20 male, 10 female) and 20 for adolescents (10 male, 10 female)—provides both in-patient and out-patient services, with an additional P60 million set aside from tobacco levies to establish further regional rehabilitation centers.

* **Date:** November 18, 2025
* **Source:** *Botswana Communications Regulatory Authority (BOCRA)*
* **Topic:** Digital Media Regulation & Youth Safety
* **Full Context & Details:**
The Botswana Communications Regulatory Authority (BOCRA) issued a formal public notice inviting stakeholder commentary on the *Digital Services Bill (2025)* framework. The proposed regulatory bill aims to update digital communications oversight by setting strict operating guidelines for internet service providers, social platforms, and digital content hosts.
Key provisions in the public notice mandate enhanced consumer protection tools, strict takedown mechanisms for non-consensual explicit media, and transparent reporting channels for cyberbullying and predatory online behavior. The authority emphasized that creating a safe, accountable digital space is essential to protecting young users from severe psychological distress, online exploitation, and toxic digital conditioning.
