Botswana Nicknames and Street Names:

Here are the Setswana terms, street names, local slang, and colloquial expressions used in Botswana (and Setswana-speaking regions) corresponding to the items mentioned in your master taxonomy.

**Part 1: Addictive Substances**

* **1. Crystal Methamphetamine:** *Mathawa*, *Tik*, *Dombo*, *Guka*, *Glass*, *Ice*.
* **2. Cocaine & Crack Cocaine:** *Coke*, *Charlie*, *Snow*, *Blow*, *Crack*, *Rock*, *Mabwe* (stones).
* **3. Khat / Mairungi:** *Miraa*, *Khat*, *Mairungi*.
* **4. Energy Drinks & High-Caffeine Products:** *Di-energy drink*, *Pill*, *Boost*, *Power*.
* **5. Heroin & Street-Cut Opiates:** *Nyaope*, *Whoonga*, *Pinch*, *Smack*.
* **6. Prescription Cough Syrups:** *Bronco*, *Lean*, *Purple Drank*, *Siropo*, *Metsi a mahibidu* (red water).
* **7. Sedatives & Dissociatives:** *Benzos*, *Mandrax*, *Pill*, *White Pipe*, *Buttons*, *Di-Pills*, *Majozi*.
* **8. Inhalants & Industrial Solvents:** *Genkem*, *Sekhomo* (glue), *Petrol*, *Metsi a sekorokoro*.
* **9. Alcohol & Illicit Spirits:** *Jwala* / *Bjalwa* (alcohol), *Kachasu*, *Musombodia*, *Khadi* (traditional Botswana brew), *Chibuku* / *Shake-Shake*, *Mojo*, *Metsi a molelo* (fire water), *Sip*.
* **10. Cannabis:** *Motokwane*, *Dagga*, *Mbanje*, *Ganja*, *Weed*, *Jet*, *Blunt*, *Zol*, *Zaza*, *Ntolo*.
* **11. Vaping & E-Cigarettes:** *Vape*, *Juul*, *E-cigar*.
* **12. Combustible Tobacco, Shisha & Snuff:** *Motsoko* (tobacco), *Bute* (snuff), *Paepe* (pipe), *Shisha*, *Hookah*, *Zol* / *Pill*.
* **13. Psychedelics:** *Mabowa* (mushrooms/shrooms), *Acid*, *Trips*.
* **14. Ultra-Processed Foods & High-Sugar:** *Dijo tsa sekgooa* (western/processed food), *Ditshokolete* / *Magwinya* (junk food/fat cakes), *Sweets*.
* **15. OTCs & Lifestyle Supplements:** *Dipilisi tsa lebenkele* (counter pills), *Di-supplement*, *Disteroid*.

**Part 3: Mental Health & Behavioral Distress — Part A**

* **27. Major Depressive Disorder:** *Kutlobotlhoko e e tseneletseng* (deep sadness/depression), *Botlhoko jwa mo mading* / *jwamowa* (pain of the soul/mind), *Fungisisa* / *Kurova nepfungwa* (excessive thinking).
* **28. Bipolar Disorder:** *Phetogo ya maikutlo* (mood changes), *Maikutlo a a pitlaganeng*.
* **29. Persistent Depressive Disorder:** *Mowa o o dropped* (persistent low spirits), *Kutlobotlhoko e e sa feleng*.
* **30. Seasonal Affective Disorder:** *Kutlobotlhoko ya paka ya ngwaga* (seasonal sadness).
* **31. Generalized Anxiety Disorder:** *Mapangachini*, *Tshogo e e sa feleng* (continuous fear/anxiety), *Pelo e e itayang ko godimo* (racing heart/anxiety).
* **32. Social Anxiety Disorder:** *Ditlhong tsa batho* (fear/shyness of people), *Tshogo ya go bua le batho*.
* **33. Panic Disorder & Panic Attacks:** *Go tlelwa ke tshogo e e potlako* (sudden panic attack), *Ho tsitsibana*.
* **34. Specific Phobias:** *Tshogo e e kgethegileng* (specific fear).
* **35. Agoraphobia:** *Tshogo ya go tswela kwantle* (fear of going outside/public spaces).
* **36. Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder:** *Mabadi a monagano* (mind scars), *Sotlego ya morago ga kotsi/ketsagalo*.
* **37. Complex PTSD:** *Mabadi a a tseneletseng a morago ga tiragalo*.
* **38. Acute Stress Disorder:** *Puso maikutlo e e tseneletseng ya nakwana*.
* **39. Adjustment Disorder:** *Thata ya go twaela phetogo* (difficulty adjusting to change).
* **40. Grief and Bereavement:** *Kutlobotlhoko ya loso* (grief of death), *Khutsafalo* (mourning).
* **41. Non-Suicidal Self-Injury:** *Go ikgobatsa* (self-harm/cutting), *Go ikgasa*.
* **42. Suicidal Ideation:** *Dikakanyo tsa go ipolaya* (thoughts of killing oneself).
* **43. Suicide Attempt Recovery:** *Go fola morago ga go leka go ipolaya*.
* **44. Anorexia Nervosa:** *Bolwetse jwa go gana go ja* (refusal to eat condition).
* **45. Bulimia Nervosa:** *Go ja ka bofefo le go tlhatsa* (binge eating and vomiting).
* **46. Binge Eating Disorder:** *Go ja mo go feteletseng* (excessive eating).
* **47. ARFID:** *Go kgetholola dijo* (extreme food selectivity).
* **48. Orthorexia Nervosa:** *Go tlhoma tlhogo mo dijong tse di phepafatseng*.
* **49. ADHD:** *Tlhoka tsebe / Tlhoka go tlhoma kelello* (inattention/hyperactivity), *Go tlhoka kelelelo*.
* **50. Autism Spectrum Disorder:** *Autism*, *Setho se e leng sa gagwe a le nosi*.
* **51. Learning Disorders:** *Thata ya go ithuta* (learning difficulty).
* **52. Intellectual Disability:** *Mabonya a tlhaloganyo* (intellectual slowness/impairment).

**Part 4: Mental Health & Behavioral Distress — Part B**

* **53. Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder:** *Mokgwa o o boelelang* (repetitive obsession/compulsion habit).
* **54. Body Dysmorphic Disorder:** *Kutlobotlhoko ka tebego ya mmele* (distress over body appearance).
* **55. Hoarding Disorder:** *Go kgobokanya dilo tse di sa feleng* (hoarding items endlessly).
* **56. Trichotillomania:** *Go somola moriri* (hair-pulling habit).
* **57. Excoriation Disorder:** *Go kgoa letlalo* (skin-picking habit).
* **58. Borderline Personality Disorder:** *Maikutlo a a sa megamegeng* (unstable emotions/personality).
* **59. Narcissistic Personality Disorder:** *Ikgogomoso e e feteletseng* (extreme narcissism/pride), *Ikgona*.
* **60. Antisocial Personality Disorder:** *Mokgwa o o kgatlhanong le setshaba* (anti-social/sociopathic behavior).
* **61. Avoidant Personality Disorder:** *Go tshaba batho/ Go inosetsa*.
* **62. Schizophrenia:** *Tlhakatlhakano ya mogopolo* (confusion of mind), *Setseno* (madness/psychosis), *Go bua le meoya* (talking to spirits).
* **63. Schizoaffective Disorder:** *Tlhakatlhakano ya maikutlo le monagano*.
* **64. Brief Psychotic Disorder:** *Tlhakatlhakano ya monagano ya nakwana*.
* **65. Substance-Induced Psychosis:** *Setseno sa diritibatsi* (drug-induced madness), *Mutoriro/Tik psychosis*.
* **66. Somatic Symptom Disorder:** *Mmele o o utlwang botlhoko ka ntlha ya mogopolo*.
* **67. Dissociative Identity Disorder:** *O na le batho ba le bantsi mo go ene* (multiple personalities).
* **68. Depersonalization / Derealization Disorder:** *Go utlwa e kete ga o teng / Go utlwa e kete lefatshe ga se la nnete*.
* **69. Insomnia Disorder:** *Go tlhoka boroko* (lack of sleep/insomnia).
* **70. Hypersomnia:** *Boroko jo bo feteletseng* (excessive sleep).
* **71. Delayed Sleep Phase Disorder:** *Nako ya boroko e e morago*.
* **72. Academic Stress Disorder:** *Pitsagano ya sekolo* (school stress/pressure).
* **73. Unemployment Depression:** *Ma1*, *Zvinhu zvakaoma*, *Kutlobotlhoko ya go tlhoka tiro* (unemployment depression), *Go goba mmu* (street wandering due to lack of employment).
* **74. Substance-Induced Mood Disorder:** *Phetogo ya maikutlo ka ntlha ya diritibatsi*.
* **75. Cultural & Spiritual Distress:** *Mweya wakaipa*, *Ngozi*, *Mowa o o thata*, *Meoya e e maswe* (bad/evil spirits/ancestral distress), *Kutlobotlhoko ya semowa* (spiritual distress).
